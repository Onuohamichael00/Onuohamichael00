## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📦 Vertex Supply Chain Performance Analysis

A SQL-based supply chain performance analysis focused on supplier, product, warehouse, shipment, profitability, and transportation performance.

Using MySQL, I developed business KPIs to evaluate inventory movement, damaged goods, transportation costs, revenue, profitability, supplier performance, product performance, warehouse performance, and monthly trends.

**Key KPIs & Insights:**

* **297K** total units received, with **289.5K** undamaged units.
* **2.52%** overall damage rate, representing approximately **7.5K damaged units**.
* **₦1.8M** in transportation costs, consuming **12.52% of revenue**.
* **₦8.5M** total inventory cost.
* **₦15.4M** potential revenue if all received units were sold.
* **₦15M** net revenue from sellable products.
* Approximately **₦400K** revenue lost due to damaged products.
* **₦6.8M** gross profit and **₦4.5M** net profit.
* Overall **30.48% net profit margin**.
* Kitchen was the strongest-performing category with approximately **₦3.5M** in net revenue.
* Metro Components was the most profitable supplier, generating approximately **₦495K** in net profit.
* Several products and two suppliers were identified as loss-making, providing clear areas for management attention.
* Kano generated the highest net revenue among warehouses despite receiving less inventory than Ibadan and Abuja.

The analysis was used to identify profitability drivers, operational inefficiencies, supplier risks, transportation cost impacts, and opportunities for improving purchasing and logistics decisions.

### 📈 FMCG Sales & Profitability Analysis

An end-to-end FMCG analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, pricing, promotions, and inventory.

I used Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX to clean and transform the data, build a structured data model, develop business KPIs, and create an interactive dashboard.

**Key Business Insights:**

* **2022 was the strongest-performing year**, with higher net sales despite lower units sold.
* Higher **Average Selling Price (ASP)** and **Average Order Value (AOV)** contributed to stronger revenue performance.
* Reduced stockouts and lower promotional dependency also supported the stronger 2022 performance.
* **2023 experienced a decline** in net sales, units sold, ASP, AOV, and average items per transaction.
* Personal Care experienced a decline in units sold from 2022 to 2023.
* BrandD was the strongest-performing brand by net sales and profit.
* The analysis helped identify the relationship between pricing, customer purchasing behavior, promotions, inventory availability, and profitability.
